Privacy Policy

Last updated October 19, 2026

Bushel does not collect, store, or share any personal data. Everything it does happens on your Mac.

What Bushel reads

Bushel reads the usage logs that AI coding tools such as Claude Code save on your Mac. It only reads the folder you choose to share with it. From those logs it counts tokens, sessions, and models. It does not read or keep the content of your conversations beyond what it needs to count usage.

What Bushel stores

Bushel saves your settings and the permission to read the folder you chose. Both stay on your Mac. You can remove them at any time by deleting the app.

What Bushel sends

Nothing. Bushel has no accounts, analytics, tracking, or advertising, and it does not send your usage data anywhere.
