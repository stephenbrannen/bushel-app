Privacy Policy

Last updated October 20, 2026

Bushel does not collect or share any personal data. Everything it does happens on your Mac.

What Bushel reads

Bushel reads the usage logs that AI coding tools such as Claude Code save on your Mac. It only reads the folder you choose to share with it. From those logs it counts tokens, sessions, and models. It does not read or keep the content of your conversations beyond what it needs to count usage.

If you set up plan limits, Claude Code's status line saves a small file in your .claude folder with your 5-hour and weekly limit percentages and reset times. Bushel reads that file to show your limits. Bushel does not write to your .claude folder; the status line command you add does.

What Bushel stores

Bushel saves daily token totals per model, and when each session started and ended, so your history survives when Claude Code deletes old transcripts. On first launch it also copies the daily totals from Claude Code's own stats file, if one is present. It does not save the text of your conversations.

It also saves your settings and the permission to read the folder you chose. All of this stays on your Mac, inside Bushel's own app storage, until you delete it.

Deleting your data

To delete Bushel's saved history, open Bushel's Settings and choose Delete Saved History.

macOS keeps an app's data after the app itself is deleted. To remove everything Bushel stores, including its settings and folder permission, delete Bushel, then delete the org.bushelapp.bushel folder in ~/Library/Containers and the folder ending in org.bushelapp.bushel in ~/Library/Group Containers.

What Bushel sends

Nothing. Bushel has no accounts, analytics, tracking, or advertising, and it does not send your usage data anywhere.
