Know where your Claude tokens go

Bushel shows your token usage from Claude Code, Claude in Xcode and Cowork, plus how much of your 5-hour and weekly limits you've used. Right in your menu bar.

Coming soon to the Mac App Store

Requires macOS 14 (Sonoma) or later.

Today, at a glance

Today's token count sits right next to the icon in your menu bar. Click it for the last 7 days, 30 days or all time.

Plan limits

Your 5-hour and weekly limits, with a countdown to each reset. The bars turn orange, then red, when you're getting close.

Every model

A daily chart split by model, and how much each one used, with input, output and cache broken out.

Your habits

Sessions, messages, tool calls, your busiest hour, your longest session and your streaks. Plus a heatmap of the last six months.

History that stays

Claude Code deletes old session files after a while. Bushel keeps your daily totals, so you don't lose them.

Private

Bushel only reads the folders you point it at and never changes anything. No account, no analytics, and nothing leaves your Mac.

Small, medium and large widgets. Each one can show your plan limits or your top models.
